# UME interface system

This release refines the incumbent identity, as confirmed by the user. Customer quote detail, owner editor and settings are working app surfaces. Their success is comprehension and completion of the next task.

## Existing brand and type

Retain the UME mark on a white header. Forest (#1c3a2f), ivory (#f4f1ea), white and muted sage carry the current identity; champagne remains an accent. The installed Plus Jakarta Sans and existing mono figure font remain the typography sources. Use one shared reading scale, tabular rupee amounts and clear alignment.

## App direction

A framed-sheet report is the refinement direction. The first question is: what is selected, what does it cost, and what happens next? The product's signature is the real room/package comparison plus the booking credit breakdown. Details are real data; never add ornamental charts, fake proof or invented figures.

## Mobile behavior

Use compact room cards, reliable native checkboxes, 44px minimum practical tap targets, 16px form input text, visible field labels and safe-area spacing. Save selection and Review & pay remain reachable at the end and in a compact bottom action bar. The bar must not cover the final content, keyboard or payment form. Full payment terms remain available without accidental truncation.

## Payment states

A mobile Pay with UPI action opens the device's payment app with the current recipient and amount. The customer completes any transaction. The separate I have made the payment action shares the selected screenshot using the native file share sheet. No duplicate underlined text-only WhatsApp control. A cancelled share or unsuccessful save must not report completion. Owner bank verification is the source of payment confirmation.

## Motion and accessibility

Use short, specific CSS transitions for button feedback or state changes; avoid movement on monetary data. Gate hover effects to actual hover-capable pointers and respect reduced motion. Preserve focus visibility, keyboard operation, status announcements, sufficient text contrast and native control semantics.
