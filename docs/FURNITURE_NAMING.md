# UME furniture names and specifications

Approved 8 November 2026. The app and generated assistant setup share [the same contract](../src/lib/furniture-contract.ts). Product titles identify the work; specifications state confirmed construction. This is UME's house vocabulary, not an industry-mandated list.

## Confirmed owner rules

- Frame type includes frames and shutters appropriate to the unit, with inside and outside laminate finish. Internal lining, shelves and partitions appear only when specified for that unit.
- SS means stainless steel. The customer name for an SS basket is **Stainless-Steel Pull-Out Basket**; grade 304 is included only when confirmed.
- The customer's name for UME's tandem-type fitting is **Soft-Close Kitchen Drawer System**. It does not promise a brand, model, drawer-side material, capacity or branded TANDEMBOX product. An expressly offered tandem/SS alternative stays an alternative rather than becoming two supplies.
- The existing kitchen default is six drawer/basket units total. Explicit job counts or exclusions take precedence, and duplicate saved six-unit instructions do not create twelve units. Later owner-approved reusable defaults can replace the original default.
- Telescopic channels remain described as telescopic channels unless soft-close channels are explicitly supplied.

## Stable wording

Generic, otherwise identical cabinet facts use the same construction-first sentence every time. A kitchen base/wall or crockery unit can be either box or frame construction; its functional title alone must not select its rate. Examples:

| Product | Confirmed rate type | Specification |
|---|---|---|
| Wardrobe Cabinet | Box type | Box-type construction for wardrobe, with inside and outside laminate finish. |
| Wardrobe Frame and Shutters | Frame type | Frame-and-shutter construction for the wardrobe, with inside and outside laminate finish. |
| Kitchen Base Cabinet | Frame type | Frame-and-shutter construction for kitchen base storage, with inside and outside laminate finish. |
| Loft Frame and Shutters | Loft - frame and shutter | Loft frame and shutters with inside and outside laminate finish. |
| Loft Storage Cabinet | Box type | Box-type loft storage cabinet with inside and outside laminate finish. |

Confirmed glass, locks, shelves, mechanisms, locations, dimensions, grades and non-laminate finishes remain in bespoke specifications. Generic clean-up must not erase them or convert them into a standard promise. A selected rate type that conflicts with explicit construction wording blocks publication for owner correction. Changing construction in Studio updates a standard sentence; custom components remain editable for review.

## Safe alias rules

- Pull-out describes movement; it does not establish stainless steel or basket construction.
- Opposite-side describes location; it does not establish tall/full-height construction.
- Mini does not establish overhead/display. A locker nickname does not prove supplied locking hardware.
- A shoe rack is distinct from a closed shoe cabinet. TV unit can describe a composition rather than just a low console.
- Pooja cabinet shutters are distinct from room-entrance doors. CNC-only, glass-only and CNC-plus-glass scopes stay separate.
- Box loft storage, loft front closures and fixed high-level panels stay separate. Parent links retain confirmed loft/unit associations.
- Parent links group work; they do not imply Above for every child. Panelling under a loft is Below it, a loft is Above its own main unit, and internal components are Inside their cabinet.
- A broad rate category is not a furniture name. Explicit Pooja entrance/cabinet CNC or glass facts determine the title; the rate key's slash does not confirm both materials.
- TV Unit with Wall Panelling identifies an owner-confirmed combined scope. A wall-sized console measurement prompts one scope/orientation check; it never justifies automatic resizing, splitting or a new charge.
- Combined wardrobe/dressing, loft/wardrobe or TV scopes are not automatically split, remeasured or repriced.

Historical records and prices are not rewritten by this vocabulary update. New/revised scope is prepared through the shared contract; customer and PDF displays use the same formatter. The catalogue v12 upgrade changes only recognized stock definitions and duplicate saved corrections, preserving all saved rates and custom definitions.

## Assistant handoff

The setup includes a usual-home scope checklist for each supplied room. It carries saved defaults and asks once about relevant unmentioned work, dimensions and counts. The owner can keep the proposed scope or list changes/exclusions. Template candidates are not automatically charged; explicit limited scope, previous exclusions, combined units and the confirmed scope of a revision take precedence. Only the saved six-unit kitchen allowance is a built-in numeric scope default. New reusable rules can be saved under Assistant instructions → Saved corrections and copied into the external assistant setup.

Use Studio → Assistant instructions → **Copy Master Gem Instructions** or **Download instructions**. Both use the currently loaded catalogue and prices. The public repository text is a baseline generated from shipped defaults, not a live rate-card sync. Do not use old archive prices to replace saved rates.

Countable items can use factual size text such as Per drawer system, Per pull-out basket or Per additional drawer with one billable unit per item and the actual quantity. This does not assert a physical size. Unknown square-foot geometry remains a clarification; suggestions outside agreed scope may keep unknown measurements until accepted.

For an owner-approved new recurring name, a supported assistant_memory_updates rule can carry the alias/scope into the saved setup. The owner must import it and refresh the external Gem/Custom GPT; no external model is silently retrained or reconfigured.
