# UME Interiors quotation workspace

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The proprietor prepares and revises private quotations for residential and commercial interior work. Customers compare packages, review room-by-room furniture scope, save preferences and arrange payment. The owner states that most customers view the app on mobile; Android Chrome is a confirmed usage context.

## Product Purpose

Make the quoted scope, package choices, prices, exclusions, payment stages and next step understandable before a customer commits. Support an initial estimate prepared from a floor plan and a later review after the actual site visit.

## Operating Context

The app runs locally and as the Cloudflare Worker quotation with D1 storage. Owner access is authenticated at /owner. Customer links use private /q/<token> URLs. The proprietor uses an external Gemini Gem or Custom GPT to turn notes into the app's reviewed JSON format.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Three existing packages retain their pricing and machine keys. Room-level choices, eligible PVC protection and optional work are calculated by the app. Recommendations remain outside the agreed scope until reviewed. Payment screenshots are evidence, not verified receipts. Bank verification remains an owner task. Unknown measurements and technical specifications must not be invented. Company and payment details are editable by the authenticated owner.

## Brand Commitments

The user confirmed preserving the existing UME logo and forest/ivory identity, while refining the experience. Use professional, plain English and familiar furniture terminology.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing logo, illustrative material/package photographs, the working comparison and pricing engine, historical arithmetic fixtures and printable quotation. No reviews, conversion results or customer-count claims have been provided for marketing use.

## Product Principles

- Show the scope and price before asking for commitment.
- Distinguish a floor-plan estimate from site-measured work.
- Explain the booking amount as part of the project advance, counted once.
- Keep saving and payment actions easy to reach on a phone.
- Preserve honest states: saved preference, screenshot shared and bank-verified payment are different events.
